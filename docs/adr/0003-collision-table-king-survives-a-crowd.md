# Collisions: arrivals that contest a square fail, and a king survives a crowd

Two or more non-king pieces arriving on the same square all die. A piece that stayed is captured by exactly one arrival but survives two or more (the arrivals die). A moving king with exactly one enemy arrival dies with it and its army merges into that enemy's. With two or more enemy arrivals, the king survives and they die. We considered "everyone dies" (simplest, as in SyncChess) but it leaves a king hit by two players in the same cycle with two claimants for its stake and army, which cannot be split. Making contested arrivals fail guarantees every captured king has exactly one captor. We rejected "stronger piece wins" (the king becomes a battering ram) and "everyone bounces back" (it breaks lazy resolution, `docs/adr/0002-order-free-lazy-resolution.md`). Kings may not move next to another king, so two kings never collide.
