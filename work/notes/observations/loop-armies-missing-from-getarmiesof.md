# An army taken over in a capture loop is not in its new owner's `getArmiesOf`

2026-11-06, from `chess-capture-loops`. `IGameRegistry.getArmiesOf(owner)` lists the armies `owner` CREATED (`_armyIdOf(owner, 1..count)`), each with its resolved owner. After a capture loop rotates ownership, the army an account took over is listed under its creator (with the new owner) and not under the account that now plays it, so the client's "my armies" (`web/src/lib/chess/armies.ts`, which reads `getArmiesOf`) never offers it to its new owner, who therefore cannot play it from the UI even though the contract lets them commit for it. Ownership is resolved lazily (`UsingGameBoard._ownerOf`), so an index by current owner is not a one-line fix: the client could filter the full registry (`getArmies`, resolved owners), or the contract could keep a per-account list of armies received in loops, appended when the loop is folded and filtered on read.
