# Moves resolve lazily from order-free accumulated facts, with no resolution transaction

Moves are validated against the start-of-cycle board, and a reveal only accumulates facts that do not depend on reveal order (each origin's destination, a per-destination arrival count, the lone arriver, whether a king arrived). A square's state is a pure function of those facts and its previous state, worked out lazily the next time something touches it; army ownership follows "captured into" pointers the same way. Chess interactions cannot simply be accumulated like the template's reference game, and the alternative, a resolution transaction after each reveal phase, needs somebody to pay for it every cycle. Lazy resolution keeps reveal order irrelevant, so gas never decides a fight, and needs no extra step. The consequence is that every rule must be expressible from counts and identities alone: no rule may make a piece bounce back to its start square.
