Play now How it works

One cycle

  1. 1

    Stake and spawn

    Buy a stake with ETH and pick a spawn point in secret. Your army appears at the end of the cycle: a king holding your stake, ringed by pawns.

  2. 2

    Commit

    Every army makes one move per cycle. You commit it as a hash, so nobody can see it while they choose theirs.

  3. 3

    Reveal together

    Everyone opens their moves, and they all resolve against the board as it was at the start of the cycle.

  4. 4

    Capture or cash out

    Take a king and its stake and army are yours. Bring your king to an exit to take your winnings home. Miss a reveal and your army freezes.

Simultaneous chess has new rules

Gas never decides a fight

On most onchain games, whoever's transaction lands first wins. Here a reveal only records facts that do not depend on order: where each piece came from, where it went, and how many pieces arrived on each square. Every square resolves lazily from those facts, with no settlement transaction for anyone to pay.

The rules live in one place only: the contract. The game runs the contract's own code in an EVM inside your browser, so what you see is exactly what the chain will do.

It keeps working without us

  • Runs from anywhere. A static site that works from IPFS or any host, with no backend.
  • Plays offline. The whole world can run on a chain inside your browser: hotseat on one device, or single player.
  • Open source. AGPL-3.0. Build and run every part from the repository.
  • Nothing tracks you. No analytics, keys stay on your device, moves stay hidden until reveal.
