---
title: 'The router drops routes once it carries more than 48 selectors, and the lobby implementation carries exactly 48'
---

Observed 2026-11-07 while building `three-piece-moves-simulation-route`. `Router10X60` (`solidity-proxy@0.5.2`, the default router of `@rocketh/router`'s `deployViaRouter`) claims 60 selectors, but its constructor writes entries 48 to 53 with `tmp_sigs_07 = tmp_sigs_08 | ...` and entries 54 and up with `tmp_sigs_07 = tmp_sigs_09 | ...` (`solc_0_8/router/Router10X60.sol`, lines 142 to 146). The fix is `tmp_sigs_08 = tmp_sigs_08 | ...` and `tmp_sigs_09 = tmp_sigs_09 | ...`. As written, a router with more than 48 selectors overwrites the selectors in positions 42 to 47 and never fills `sigs_08`, so some routed calls revert `UNKNOWN_METHOD`. Nothing catches this at deploy time. The world's `Game` router has 46 selectors and `GameLobbyImplementation` has exactly 48. Adding the two Simulation functions (`isLegalWholeMove`, `legalMovesAlongside`) took the lobby to 50, and 12 table and lobby tests then failed with `UNKNOWN_METHOD` (`Lobby.test.ts`, `Tables.test.ts`, `GasBudget.test.ts`'s table reveal), while every world test still passed. So any new selector on any route breaks every table. `three-piece-moves-table-toggle`'s getter will hit the same wall. 0.5.2 is the latest published version of `solidity-proxy`.
